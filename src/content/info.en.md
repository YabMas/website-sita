I work with one of the oldest crafts we know: the natural tanning of hides. With materials from nature itself — tree bark, smoke, brain, and time.

The hides I work with come from the food chain. What would otherwise be lost is given a new chapter through this process.

I work slowly and with attention. Every step takes time, because I don't want to force anything that nature itself does not hurry.

For me this work is not only about the material, but about what it evokes. A memory of how close we once lived to nature — and perhaps still are.

## The craft

I work with one of the oldest ways of preserving skin: the natural tanning of sheepskins and hides. A slow process, in which time, attention, and material do the work together.

I tan with what nature itself offers: tree bark, smoke, sometimes eggs or other plant-based and natural materials. No synthetic substances, no accelerators. Only processes that have existed for generations.

Every hide asks for its own approach. No two are the same, and that is exactly where the value of this work lies for me.

## Working with attention

This is not a hurried process. It takes time to understand a hide, to treat it, and to let it become what it can be.

I work with my hands, my senses, and my attention to the material. What emerges is not a standardised product, but a hide that keeps its own character.

## Why I do this

For me this work is not only about technique, but about memory.

A memory of a way of living in which material was not separate from nature, but came directly from it. Where things were made, used, and worn with care.

That connection is something I try to make tangible in my work — not as something from the past, but as something that still exists in how we touch, feel, and live.

## My workspace

I work from my studio, where hide, time, and material come together. There, each piece receives the attention it needs to become what it wants to be.

## Naturally tanned

The hides I work with come from the food chain. For many of them there is no longer a purpose; without further processing they would be thrown away. By tanning them naturally, they are given a new life — not out of a wish to make something new, but to preserve what was already there.

These hides are fully naturally tanned and contain no chrome or other tanning chemicals. People who react to chemically tanned hides sometimes find natural hides a pleasant alternative.
